The code of the board. More guidelines than rules.

Special Orders

  • Castling: the captain slides two squares toward a rook, which hops over him. Neither may have moved, and no crossing a square under fire.
  • En passant: a deckhand that just leapt two squares may be taken as if it had stepped one.
  • Promotion: a deckhand reaching the far shore is made a flagship (queen).
  • Parley: stalemate, repetition, fifty quiet moves or too few pieces ends the fight in a draw.
