## 1. Your first game in 60 seconds

1. Open the game. You land on **The Fleet**.
2. On the first card, **Joshamee Gibbs**, press **Challenge**.
3. You arrive at the **Quarterdeck**. Under **Terms of Battle**, pick your colour and time, then press **New battle** (the game also starts as soon as you press Challenge).
4. **Tap a piece**, and small dots show where it can go. **Tap a dot** to move.
5. The captain replies automatically. Keep playing until the game ends.
6. Win and you earn a badge and unlock the next captain.

## 2. The four pages

Use the navigation bar at the top. Each page has its own web address (`#/fleet`, `#/play`, `#/treasury`, `#/articles`), so the browser Back button works.

| Page | What it is |
|---|---|
| **The Fleet** | Your rank card and all 20 captains. Pick who to fight. |
| **Quarterdeck** | The game board, clocks, opponent card, move list and battle settings. |
| **Treasury** | The 9-ship rank ladder and your 20 badges. |
| **Ship's Articles** | A short rulebook: what each piece does and the special rules. |

### The Fleet in detail

- **Rank card (top):** your current ship, your rank, wins/draws/losses, and a rope progress bar towards your next promotion.
- **Captain cards:** each shows the captain's emblem, title, threat bar (1–20), taunt and the ship badge you'll earn.
  - **Challenge**: start a battle.
  - **Rematch**: appears on captains you have already beaten.
  - **🔒 Defeat #N**: locked until you beat captain N.
  - **⚓ Next on your voyage**: marks the next captain to beat.
- **Chart all seas (unlock everyone):** unlocks all captains so you can fight in any order. Press again to lock them again. Your badges are not affected.
- **Burn the logbook (reset):** erases all progress. You must press it **twice** within 3 seconds to confirm.

## 3. Playing a battle

### Making a move

- **Tap or click** one of your pieces. It gets a blue outline and its legal destinations are marked.
  - A **glowing blue dot** is a normal move.
  - A **red ring** around an enemy piece is a capture.
- **Tap a marked square** to move there.
- To change your mind, tap a different piece of yours, or tap an empty unmarked square.
- You cannot move while the captain is thinking. The Battle panel shows "<captain> is plotting…".

### Special moves

- **Castling:** select your king and tap the square two steps toward a rook.
- **En passant:** if available, the capture square is marked as a normal capture target.
- **Promotion:** when a pawn reaches the last rank it **automatically becomes a queen**. There is no piece picker.

### What you see on the board

- **Gold shading:** the squares of the last move played.
- **Red glow:** a king in check.
- **Evaluation bar (left of the board):** a rough read of who is ahead. The ivory part is White's share and the dark part is Black's, and it follows the board's orientation. It is a quick indicator, not an oracle.
- **Captured pieces and material:** shown on each player strip with a "+N" for the side ahead.

### The naval duel strip

Above the board, **your ship** (your current rank ship) faces the **enemy's black flag**.

- The **hull bars** shrink as each side loses material.
- A ship **shakes** when it captures a piece.
- When the battle ends, the **loser's ship sinks**.

This strip is visual feedback only. It does not change the rules.

### The opponent card

On the Quarterdeck, the **Your Opponent** card shows the captain you are facing, their title, their threat level, which badge you will earn, and their taunt.

## 4. Clocks and time controls

Choose under **Terms of Battle → Time on the glass**:

| Option | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 1+0 | 1 minute, no increment |
| 3+2 | 3 minutes, +2 seconds per move |
| 5+0 | 5 minutes (default) |
| 10+0 | 10 minutes |
| 15+10 | 15 minutes, +10 seconds per move |
| ∞ | No clocks |

- Clocks start after the first move of the game.
- The active clock turns red.
- Run out of time and you lose. The captain's clock also counts the real time they spend thinking.
- Changes to colour or time apply the next time you start a battle.

## 5. Battle buttons

On the **The Battle** panel:

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Takeback** | Undoes your last move **and** the captain's reply, so it is your turn again. Not available while the captain is thinking or after the game ends. |
| **Flip board** | Rotates the board view. |
| **Offer parley** | Offers a draw. The captain only accepts if the game has gone on for at least 16 half-moves **and** they are not clearly ahead by their own evaluation. Otherwise they refuse and you can keep playing. |
| **Strike colours** | Resign. Counts as a loss. |

On **Terms of Battle**:

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Your colour** | White, Black or Random. Applies on the next battle. |
| **Time on the glass** | See [Clocks](#4-clocks-and-time-controls). |
| **New battle** | Restart against the same captain. |
| **To the fleet** | Back to the captain list. |
| **Midnight / Dusk** | Switch between the darkest theme and a lighter dusk-blue theme. |

## 6. Winning, drawing and losing

When a battle ends, a stamped panel appears.

| Result | How it happens | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| **Victory!** | You checkmate the captain, or the captain's flag falls | Badge awarded (first time), next captain unlocked, possible promotion |
| **Parley (draw)** | Stalemate, threefold repetition, fifty moves without a capture or pawn move, too little material to mate, or an accepted draw offer | Draw recorded. No badge. |
| **Sunk!** | You are checkmated, you resign, or your flag falls | Loss recorded. Rematch offered. |

On the result panel you can press **Next captain** (after a win), **Rematch** or **The fleet**.

Beating a captain you have already beaten does not change your badge count or rank. Only your win total goes up.

## 7. Ranks, ships and promotion

Your **ship is your rank**. It depends only on how many *different* captains you have beaten.

| Captains beaten | Rank | Ship |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Castaway | Wicked Wench |
| 2 | Deckhand | HMS Interceptor |
| 5 | Buccaneer | HMS Providence |
| 7 | Privateer | HMS Dauntless |
| 10 | Corsair | HMS Endeavour |
| 12 | Captain | Queen Anne's Revenge |
| 15 | Commodore | Silent Mary |
| 17 | Pirate Lord | Black Pearl |
| 20 | Admiral of the Seven Seas | Flying Dutchman |

- You can never lose rank.
- When a win crosses a threshold, a **PROMOTED!** panel shows your new ship on the result screen.
- Your rank and ship are shown on your player strip during every battle and on the Fleet and Treasury pages.
- Ranks give **no gameplay advantage**. They are a title and a trophy.

## 8. Badges and the Treasury

There is one badge per captain, 20 in total.

- A badge is a round medal showing a moonlit scene with the **ship of that captain's band**, a ribbon with that ship's name, laurel leaves, and a seal with the captain's emblem.
- Unearned badges are shown greyed out with "???".
- The badge name appears on the Treasury card (for example, "Kraken's Eye").

The Treasury has two sections: the **Rank ladder** (all nine ships, the ones you haven't reached are greyed out) and **Captain badges**.

Badge bands: captains 1–2 (Interceptor), 3–5 (Providence), 6–7 (Dauntless), 8–10 (Endeavour), 11–12 (Queen Anne's Revenge), 13–15 (Silent Mary), 16–17 (Black Pearl), 18–20 (Flying Dutchman).

## 9. Progress, saving and resetting

- Progress is saved automatically in your browser (`localStorage`, key `caribbean`) after every battle.
- It is stored **per browser and per device**. There is no account, cloud save or export.
- Clearing site data, or using private/incognito mode, will lose it.
- To reset: use **Burn the logbook** on the Fleet page (press twice), or clear the site's storage in your browser.

## 10. Chess rules reminder

If you are new to chess, the **Ship's Articles** page has a summary. In brief:

| Piece (ship's name) | Moves |
|---|---|
| King (The Captain) | One square in any direction. Must never be left in check. |
| Queen (The Flagship) | Any distance along rank, file or diagonal. |
| Rook (The Fortress Cannon) | Any distance along ranks and files. |
| Bishop (The Navigator) | Any distance along diagonals; stays on one colour. |
| Knight (The Privateer) | L-shape, two squares one way and one across. Jumps over pieces. |
| Pawn (The Deckhand) | Forward one (two from its starting square), captures diagonally, promotes on the last rank. |

The goal is **checkmate**: attack the enemy king so that it cannot escape.

## 11. Tips for beating the captains

- **Start on a long clock (10+0 or ∞)** while you learn each captain. Switch to faster clocks later for a challenge.
- **Captains 1–8 hang pieces.** Look for free captures before each move, and check that your own pieces are not undefended.
- **From captain 9 up they see short tactics.** Before moving, ask "what can they capture or fork after this?"
- **Captains 16–20 rarely blunder material.** Aim for a solid position, trade when ahead, and beware of their long-term pressure.
- **Use Takeback to learn.** Experiment, then undo and compare.
- **Use the evaluation bar and hull bars** as a quick gauge of who is ahead.
- **The engine has no opening book or endgame knowledge.** It can be outplayed in long endgames and by sound opening play.

## 12. FAQ

**Is this multiplayer?**
No. It is a single-player game against a built-in AI. "Online-style" refers to the interface only.

**Is the "Threat 1–20" a real chess rating?**
No. It is just a difficulty ladder for this game.

**Can I choose which piece a pawn promotes to?**
Not yet. It always promotes to a queen.

**Can I play with the keyboard or drag pieces?**
Not yet. Tap or click a piece, then tap or click a destination.

**Can I skip ahead to a hard captain?**
Yes: press **Chart all seas (unlock everyone)** on the Fleet page.

**Why did a captain refuse my draw offer?**
They accept only after enough moves and when they are not clearly better.

**Does it work offline?**
Not out of the box. See [INSTRUCTIONS.md](INSTRUCTIONS.md#run-fully-offline).

**Why do the captains have so many ships on their badges but no ships of their own?**
By design: ships are *your* rank and the badge bands. No captain owns a ship.

## 13. Troubleshooting

| Problem | Likely cause and fix |
|---|---|
| Board is empty or nothing is clickable | The chess library could not load (offline or blocked CDN). Reconnect, allow `cdnjs.cloudflare.com`, or follow the offline instructions. |
| Fonts look plain | Google Fonts blocked. The game falls back to a system serif font and still works. |
| Some emoji emblems look flat or black | Your OS lacks colour emoji for that symbol. Try a different browser or update the system. |
| A captain takes several seconds to move | Normal for the hardest captains (search time cap is up to about 4.4 seconds). |
| Progress disappeared | Site data was cleared, or you were in private mode, or you used a different browser or device. |
| Page is slow on an old phone | The artwork uses SVG filters. Try a newer browser or close other tabs. |
| Animations bother me | The game respects your system's "reduce motion" setting. |
