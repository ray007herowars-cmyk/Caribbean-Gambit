# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Caribbean Gambit to be a friendly place to learn, play and build. Everyone who takes part in this project, whether through issues, pull requests or discussions, is expected to help keep it welcoming, regardless of age, background, identity, experience level or skill at chess.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, especially with beginners.
- Give and accept feedback constructively. Criticise ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.
- Credit other people's work.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or discriminatory language or jokes
- Personal attacks or sustained disruption of discussions
- Publishing someone else's private information
- Sexualised content or unwelcome attention
- Spam, or posting other people's copyrighted material without permission

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block repeat offenders. To report a problem, use GitHub's **Report content** option on the item, or contact a maintainer privately through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled in confidence.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces and when someone is representing the project in public.

This code of conduct is adapted from the ideas of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
